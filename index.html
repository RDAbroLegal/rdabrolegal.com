About

Rita D. Abro, Attorney at Law

After nearly a decade in Big Law, I built RD Abro Legal to give companies and individuals the kind of tax counsel usually reserved for institutional clients — without the institutional overhead.

My career has been spent structuring and defending tax positions: building entities and transactions the right way from the start, keeping compliance defensible year over year, and, when necessary, standing between clients and the IRS. That range — planning, transactions, and controversy under one roof — means advice that accounts for how positions are actually examined, not just how they read on paper.

“The best tax position is one that never needs defending — but is built as if it will.”
